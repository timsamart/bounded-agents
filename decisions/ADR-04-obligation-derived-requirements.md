# ADR-04. Obligation-derived requirements {#adr-04}

**Status:** Accepted (edition 0.2.0 draft)  
**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Deciders:** Lead author (Timotheos Samartzidis)  
**Argued in:** `chapters/1.2-constraints.md`

**In plain terms:** Write requirements from the legal or business obligation, not by mapping a vendor control catalogue.

## Context

Catalogue mapping is fast and auditor-familiar. It also produces controls that satisfy a citation and stop nothing - with the gap marked closed.

## Decision

Derive every requirement from a named obligation. Never start from a vendor control catalogue.

## Why not the alternative

**Rejected:** Map obligations onto a vendor catalogue for speed and familiar labels.

You inherit someone else's questions. The matrix looks complete while the mechanism may not exist.

## What changes if you follow this

Appendix A rows carry an argument someone can contest. Design reviews take longer; audits get cheaper because the story is coherent.

## Cost

About two weeks of careful derivation versus two weeks of theatre that fails later. Pay upfront.

## Reopen when

A regulation is shown to require a named control product rather than evidence of an outcome.
